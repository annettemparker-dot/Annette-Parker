About

For 15+ years I've helped brands find their voice, build the partnerships that matter, and grow in ways that actually stick. I've done that work inside one of the world's top sports agencies, embedded with founder-led startups, and everywhere in between.

These days I bring that experience to companies as a Fractional VP of Marketing — senior brand leadership, without the full-time overhead. I'm most energized working with founder-led brands and growth-stage companies that are ready to move from instinct to strategy.

My background spans nearly a decade at CAA Sports, where I originated and grew Eastside Golf from a new client to a retainer relationship — brokering $500K+ in partnerships — before transitioning in-house as their Head of Marketing.
